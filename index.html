Google Play screenshot sizes

Play is stricter about ratio than pixel count: every side must fall between 320 and 3840 pixels, and no side can be more than twice the other. That 2:1 ceiling is why Apple's tall 9:19.5 iPhone files cannot simply be reused on Play.

How it works

  1. Drop in one or more screenshots, or paste from the clipboard.
  2. Write a headline and supporting line for each, above or below the image.
  3. Pick a theme, background pattern, device frame and typeface.
  4. Tick the store sizes you need and export — everything arrives as a zip, foldered by store.

Questions

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Every resize happens on an HTML canvas inside your own browser. No image leaves your device, there is no account, and there is no server to upload to.

Why was my screenshot rejected for an alpha channel?

Both stores reject images containing transparency. A PNG exported from a browser canvas is 32-bit RGBA even when every pixel is fully opaque, so switching the export format to JPEG clears the rejection.

Do I need a separate screenshot for every iPhone size?

Usually not. Apple scales the largest set down to smaller device classes, so the 6.9-inch files plus an iPad set cover most listings.

Can I use a device frame around my screenshot?

Yes, with limits. Apple allows an iPhone bezel on iPhone sizes and marketing captions on top (guideline 2.3.3), but rejects an iPhone frame on iPad and Android chrome on iOS (2.3.10). Play allows stylized phone screenshots, asks that taglines stay modest, and does not want device imagery on the 1024×500 feature graphic. Storefront drops a frame on any size that store would refuse.
